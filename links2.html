Is it true that we should eliminate the Electoral College due to the changing times?

We seem to know more about our candidates than our electors. Is it fair to continue to elect someone for the most powerful position in the world the way our country always has, or do we have room for reform? Is it reasonable to continue making sure that smaller states are not left out of the decision for President or should we learn something from the fact that an American presidential candidate has lost five times due to winning the popular vote but losing the electoral college?
